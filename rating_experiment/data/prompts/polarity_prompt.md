In this study we are selecting linguistic stimuli and we need evaluations on a series of
words. For each word, rate your level of agreement with the following statement:

"I relate this word to positive or negative feelings in myself."

The scale ranges from 1 to 7, as follows:
Strongly Disagree <- 1      2      3      4      5      6      7 -> Strongly Agree

Keep in mind that the value of "1" corresponds to Strongly Disagree and value of "7"
to Strongly Agree. Please feel free to use the whole range of values provided. When
making your ratings, try to be as accurate as possible, but do not spend too much time
on any one word.

In each line of the output write only the word and its rating in a tuple like this:
('word', INT_SCORE).
